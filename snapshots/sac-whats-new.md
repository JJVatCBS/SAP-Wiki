Performance Guardrails for Calendar Events	You can now work with large processes in the calendar without risking browser slowdowns or crashes. The system now limits the number of events in the following areas:
In the list workspace, results are capped at 35,000 events, with guidance to filter further.
In the Gantt chart, a placeholder appears when your visible expanded events exceed 1,000.
In the calendar wizard, you cannot generate more than 35,000 events at once.

Random Sampling Support for 0 or Unbooked Baseline
In compass simulations, random sampling is now supported for drivers that have a zero or unbooked baseline.
This enhancement allows planners to account for uncertainty and simulate model accounts with no historical activity or interrupted by strategic changes.
