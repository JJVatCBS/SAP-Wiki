## Disclaimer
In order to use the Data Product Generator for SAP Business Data Cloud, you need to be
on a certain version of your BW system, lift your BW system into the private cloud component
of SAP Business Data Cloud, and have an SAP Business Data Cloud cockpit tenant with an SAP
Datasphere system in the formation.

To use more advanced functionalities, such as sharing data products to SAP Databricks, you
might require further modules of SAP Business Data Cloud, such as the SAP Databricks
capability, enabled within your SAP Business Data Cloud formation.

This documentation applies if you're on one of the following BW systems:

- SAP BW 7.5 SP24 or higher ([SAP Business Warehouse - Help Portal Documentation](https://help.sap.com/docs/SAP_NETWEAVER_750/ff18034f08af4d7bb33894c2047c3b71/b2e50138fede083de10000009b38f8cf.html))

- SAP BW/4HANA 2021 SP04 or higher ([SAP BW/4HANA 2021 - Help Portal Documentation](https://help.sap.com/docs/SAP_BW4HANA?version=2021.13&state=DRAFT))

- SAP BW/4HANA 2023 SP00 or higher ([SAP
BW/4HANA 2023 - Help Portal Documentation](https://help.sap.com/docs/SAP_BW4HANA?state=DRAFT))

Caution

For SAP BW/4HANA 2023, the Data Product Generator is technically available from SP00.
However, productive usage is only supported and recommended from SP04 or higher. On
SP00–SP03, use the Data Product Generator for prototyping or functional testing
only. There will be no further deliveries of new functionality or performance
improvements on SAP BW/4HANA 2023 SP00–SP03. For productive usage, upgrade to SAP
BW/4HANA 2023 SP04 or higher. For more information, see
SAP Note [3593519](https://me.sap.com/notes/3593519).

Please note, that you can only use functions in the SAP BW/4HANA Cockpit, such as the Data
Subscriptions app, on SAP BW/4HANA systems. Equivalent functions are offered within the
RSDPS transaction in the SAP GUI on SAP BW 7.5
systems.

Some features of the Data Product Generator might only be available on higher support packages
than the minimum system requirements for the Data Product Generator itself. For detailed
information on the specific features and their required support packages, see [What's New (Release Information)](207bd08e56a8487e88173373fd2f46b8.html).
