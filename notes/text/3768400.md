# 3768400 – End of Extended Maintenance by end of 2030 for SAP BW NetWeaver Cloud

Version 3 · freigegeben 10.09.2026 · Komponente BW · [in SAP for Me öffnen](https://me.sap.com/notes/3768400)

SAP Note

3768400 - End of Extended Maintenance by end of 2030 for SAP BW NetWeaver Cloud
Component: BW (SAP Business Warehouse), Version: 3, Released On: 10.09.2026

## Symptom

End of Extended Maintenance by end of 2030 for SAP BW NetWeaver Cloud where customer needs more time for their BW
Modernization journey within SAP Business Data Cloud.

## Other Terms

Information on SAP BW 7.50, private edition, SAP BW NetWeaver Cloud, transition option required.

## Reason and Prerequisites

Customer with complex scenarios need more time for their BW Modernization journey within SAP Business Data Cloud.
To take advantage of the transition option, please note the following prerequisites:
Modernization Option is available only for systems operated as private cloud systems
DatabaseRequirement: SAP HANA 2.0 will be the only supported database for this offering.
EHP Requirement: Customers must ensure their ERP system is at ABAP Kernel 7.54 with a deployed SAP enhancement package 7
(ABAP 740) or SAP enhancement package 8 (ABAP 750).
Operating System Requirement: Systems are operated on Linux x86.

## Solution

SAP offers an SAP Business Warehouse, private cloud edition for SAP NetWeaver transition option to support customers with large
and complex SAP BW NetWeaver 7.50 landscapes who may not be able to fully move to SAP Business Data Cloud by 2030.
To better support customers operating diverse
and industry‑specific SAP landscapes, the scope of the transition option is being extended to additional product versions.
These product versions are included to address complex scenarios where customers rely on integrated or add‑on components that cannot be
timeframe as documented in note 3590297.
This SAP Note outlines the specific applicability, technical prerequisites, and known constraints for SAP Business Warehouse, private
cloud edition for SAP NetWeaver.
Known limitations
JAVA Application Server will not be supported beyond 2030.
Secondary database connections (e.g. in the context of Open Hub) will not be supported beyond 2030.
Add-on and additional components
The following add-ons will also be part of the transition option:
SAP Business, Planning & Consolidation (BPC)
SAP Enterprise Management, specifically: SEM-BCS, SEM-BPS, SEM-CPM
Subscription Information
The SAP Business Warehouse, private cloud edition for SAP
NetWeaver, transition option will be available for subscription from January 1,
2031, with a minimum duration of 12 months. Subscriptions will end no later than December 31, 2033.
