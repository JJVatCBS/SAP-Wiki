       SAP Note


  2881788 - End of SAP Business Suite 7 mainstream maintenance
  Component: XX-SER-REL-ERP (Miscellaneous > Service Messages > Business Suite and SAP S/4HANA Release
  Information > Release Information for SAP ERP), Version: 7, Released On: 01.10.2026


  Symptom
End of SAP Business Suite 7 mainstream maintenance




  Other Terms
Information on maintenance of SAP Business Suite 7 required




  Reason and Prerequisites
This note describes the maintenance strategy and maintenance phases of SAP Business Suite 7




  Solution
To enable planning security and support a sufficient transition phase from SAP Business Suite 7 to SAP S/4HANA, SAP provides
mainstream maintenance for core applications of SAP Business Suite 7 software (including SAP ERP 6.0, SAP Customer Relationship
Management 7.0, SAP Supply Chain Management 7.0, and SAP Supplier Relationship Management 7.0 applications and SAP Business Suite
powered by SAP HANA) until December 31, 2027.

This long-term commitment applies for the SAP Business Suite 7 core applications on the respective latest three Enhancement Packages
(EhPs) (*), corresponding add-on applications, and for embedded SAP NetWeaver and SAP Business Warehouse components, unless an end of
maintenance for such solutions had been already announced earlier, or there is already a follow-on release in place. See the SAP
Release Brochure for more details.

(*) After 2025, SAP will provide mainstream maintenance for the latest three EhPs only. The SAP Business Suite 7 core applications
without any EhP or on a lower EhP than the latest three EhPs require an upgrade to a more recent EhP to receive maintenance.

The table below (**) documents all EhPs for which maintenance is currently committed after 2025.

This new maintenance strategy as well as the timeline below prevails over any other deviating statement regarding SAP Business Suite
7 maintenance phase(s) in any other SAP document.

In case SAP makes available SAP Business Suite 7 add-ons provided by SAP Partners or other SAP Business Suite 7 third-party
solutions, these add-ons or third-party solutions follow the SAP’s Partner or third-party vendor maintenance strategy and might be
excluded from this SAP Business Suite 7 maintenance long-term commitment.

Technical limitations and/or (general) restrictions might require an upgrade to newer releases of SAP Business Suite 7 related
applications, enhancement packages, add-ons or other technical components.

Maintenance timeline for SAP Business Suite 7:

     Until December 31, 2027: Mainstream maintenance.
     Default: From January 1, 2028: Customer-specific maintenance.
     Optional: From January 1, 2028 to December 31, 2030: Extended maintenance. This 3-year extended maintenance phase comes at an
     additional fee on top of the respective support fee. For SAP ERP, private edition/tailored option, extended maintenance is
     included in the subscription fee and does not require any additional fee.
     From January 1, 2031 only customer-specific maintenance will be offered.

Please be aware, extended maintenance is not available for a subset of the customer installation only. It always applies to the full
landscape of SAP Business Suite 7 core applications and all respective SAP Business Suite 7 Add-Ons.

Maintenance timelines for non-SAP database and operating systems are not affected by the maintenance strategy of SAP Business Suite
7. These timlines are set by the respective providers. For detailed timlines please refer to the SAP Product Availability Matrix and
check if the respective OS/DB/Kernel configuration is listed in the technical release information tab. Specifically for database-
related information please also refer to SAP Notes such as the following:

Remarks about non-unicode/unicode support:

     SAP Business Suite 7 Core applications including EhPs on SAP Netweaver version up to and including 7.40 support non-unicode and
     unicode deployments.
     SAP Business Suite 7 Core applications including EhPs on SAP Netweaver 7.50 or later support unicode deployemnts only.
     For more details please refer to Knowledge Base Article 1896539 and SAP Note 1648480.
     The table below (**) documents all EhPs for which maintenance is currently committed after 2025 including non-unicode/unicode
     deployment support.

Other SAP notes or documents published before February 4, 2020 and describing a different SAP Business Suite 7 maintenance strategy
will be replaced by this note (2881788) and may become invalid in whole or in part.

This note reflects the current status of planning and may be updated at short notice.
