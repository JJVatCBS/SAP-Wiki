# 2741041 – Maintenance for SAP NetWeaver 7.X Business Warehouse

Version 12 · freigegeben 15.04.2026 · Komponente BW · [in SAP for Me öffnen](https://me.sap.com/notes/2741041)

SAP Note

2741041 - Maintenance for SAP NetWeaver 7.X Business Warehouse
Component: BW (SAP Business Warehouse), Version: 12, Released On: 15.04.2026

## Symptom

In the SAP Product Availability Matrix (PAM), the length of maintenance for SAP NetWeaver 7.X Business Warehouse is not
easily identifiable.

## Other Terms

SAP NetWeaver, Java, 7.X, 7.30, 7.40, 7.50, maintenance, maintenance strategy, mainstream maintenance, extension of
maintenance, 2017, 2020, support packages

## Reason and Prerequisites

The SAP Product Availability Matrix (PAM) describes the SAP software product versions (SPVs). In particular, the PAM
contains the availability and maintenance periods for the versions of SAP software products.
SAP NetWeaver 7.X Business Warehouse, for example SAP NetWeaver 7.40 Business Warehouse, is not itself a product
version.
Licenses for SAP NetWeaver 7.x Business Warehouse (for example, for 7.40) are not offered directly; they are offered
indirectly via the product that you use.
The maintenance period of the product also determines the length of maintenance for SAP NetWeaver 7.X Business
Warehouse, so SAP NetWeaver 7.40 Business Warehouse, for example.

## Solution

SAP NetWeaver 7.X Business Warehouse can be used in two ways:
Case A: As an "embedded data warehouse" in a "standalone" product version containing SAP NetWeaver Business
Warehouse (for example, SAP Business Suite 7)
Case B: As a standalone version of SAP NetWeaver Business Warehouse (Business Warehouse hub usage of SAP
NetWeaver).
In the first case, case A, the maintenance cycle is defined by the maintenance of the "standalone" product version - SAP
Business Suite, for example (for more information, see SAP Note 1648480).
In the second case, case B, the following applies:

In this second case, case B, SAP NetWeaver 7.X Business Warehouse is used as part of SAP NetWeaver 7.X. This means that
the maintenance cycle of the (complete) SAP NetWeaver product (including Java) also determines the maintenance cycle of
SAP NetWeaver 7.X Business Warehouse.
The mainstream maintenance of SAP NetWeaver 7.3X and 7.40 ends on DEC/31/2020. We therefore recommend that, in the
second case, case B, you plan and implement the upgrade to SAP NetWeaver 7.5 or the migration to SAP BW/4HANA at the
appropriate time. After mainstream maintenance, customer-specific maintenance is offered for the product versions SAP
NetWeaver 7.3X and 7.40. See SAP Note 52505.
Maintenance for SAP NetWeaver 7.5 Business Warehouse has been extended because maintenance of the product SAP
NetWeaver 7.5 has been extended in general: As mentioned in the PAM , main-maintenance is until the end of 2027. As in the
Business Suite, you can then get a maintenance extension (for a fee) until the end of 2030.

In addition, SAP BW, SAP BW/4HANA Maintenance FAQ answers additional questions.
Note that the "Business Warehouse" (for example, SAP NetWeaver 7.40 Business Warehouse) is not the same as the software
component version SAP_BW (for example, SCV "SAP BW 7.40" (Version 740 of SAP_BW)). The "Business Warehouse"
application is basically viewed as an application of the full SAP NetWeaver stack - a solution from the SAP NetWeaver
product. This also applies if parts of SAP NetWeaver (for example, BI Java) are not used.
(Since the software component version SAP_BW may also be used in the SAP Business Suite ("Data warehousing embedded"
), not only the end of maintenance of SAP NetWeaver is relevant for the SCV SAP_BW; the end of maintenance for the suite is
also relevant for this application.)
